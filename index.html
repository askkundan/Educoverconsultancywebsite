FAQ

Questions worth asking

What can an education loan cover?

Coverage varies by lender and loan terms. Ask the lender for its eligible expense list.

Do I need collateral or a co-applicant?

Requirements vary based on lender, loan amount and applicant profile.

Which documents do I need?

Academic, admission, identity and financial documents may be requested. The lender confirms its checklist.

Can I apply before admission?

Some lenders may offer preliminary assessment; requirements differ.

Does Educover charge students?

Confirm the current service-fee policy directly with Educover.
